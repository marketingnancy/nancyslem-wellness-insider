1. Easy to start: one button

Nobody wants a manual when they're in the mood. One button, starting at the gentlest level, means you press, relax, and enjoy. Every extra button and mode is one more thing to figure out first.

Toys in this category run from about $40 to $189. Under $100 buys something that does the job, without extras you may never touch.

A toy that looks like something you'd happily leave on a shelf is a toy you reach for more often. It doesn't change the score. It's a bonus, and only one toy earned it.
