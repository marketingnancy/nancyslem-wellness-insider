What to look for in a first toy

A first purchase goes more smoothly when you know what matters. These are the five things we looked for.

  1. Made with beginners in mind. A toy designed for first-timers has simple controls and a gentle first setting, so there's nothing to work out before you begin.
  2. Body-safe silicone. Soft, body-safe silicone is comfortable against the skin, has some give, and is easy to clean. A hard plastic body is rigid and has no flex.
  3. A rechargeable battery. Built-in charging means no replacement batteries to buy, and no power that fades as batteries run down.
  4. A warranty and a guarantee. Support from the maker means you're covered if the toy isn't what you expected, or if it stops working. That matters most with a low-cost toy, where some owners report units cutting out early.
  5. Customer reviews. We read what owners say about each toy, complaints included, because it shows how a toy holds up over time.

Why quality is worth paying for

Whatever you choose touches your skin, so a first toy deserves a little more care than the cheapest one on the shelf. Body-safe materials, a rechargeable battery and real support from the maker are worth paying for.

Which type is which

Bullet

A small, simple toy, about the size of a lipstick. It's compact, affordable and easy to store.

Suction (air-pulse)

A toy that uses pulses of air instead of vibration. The Lem and the Puff work this way.

Wand

A larger toy with a rounded head, made for broad, strong vibration. It suits people who already know what they like.

Rabbit

A toy with two motors, which gives it the most settings to learn. It's better suited to a later purchase.

Getting started

These steps work with any of the toys on this page.

  1. Start on the lowest setting. You can always move up from there.
  2. Use water-based lubricant. It's safe to use with silicone toys.
  3. Clean the toy after each use. Warm water and mild soap or a toy cleaner work well, and you can let it dry fully.
  4. Store it somewhere dry and private. A drawer or a pouch does the job.
  5. Give yourself time. Suction feels different from vibration, so it can take a little while to get used to.
