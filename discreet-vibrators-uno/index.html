What actually arrives at your door

A plain brown cardboard box. Nothing printed on it, and nothing that says what's inside. That's how every standard Hello Nancy order ships.

It's the standard worth looking for in any order: no logo, no product name and no pictures on the outside.

In the US, Hello Nancy orders take around 3 to 7 business days, and shipping is free over $65 (below that, checkout shows the fee). If you're out most days, order for a week when you'll be home for the delivery, so the box isn't left by the door.

When it arrives, flatten the box and put it out with the rest of your recycling, like any other delivery.

What your card statement shows

Your bank lists every charge under a name. For a Hello Nancy order, the line reads SP HELLO NANCY or SP RCH NANCY, so you'll know it when you see it.

That line shows wherever your account is read: the banking app, an emailed statement or a paper one. Many banks also send an alert the moment a card is used, to a lock screen or an email address.

Check where those go before you buy. If you share an account, a statement or a screen with someone, a card that only you see is the simplest answer.

Your inbox, your phone and your browser

The box and the statement are the big two. Three smaller things are worth a minute before you order:

  1. A private window. Shopping in a private browsing window, or signing out afterwards, means the visit isn't saved in that browser's history.
  2. An email address only you read. Your order confirmation and shipping updates go there.
  3. A phone number only you read, with previews off. Delivery texts can appear on a locked screen, and turning message previews off takes a moment in your phone's settings.
